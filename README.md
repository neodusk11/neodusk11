<h3 align="center">Small tools, sharp edges, no drama.</h3>

I spend most of my days in Jupyter notebooks, turning messy CSVs into something a decision can be made from. Lately I've been drawn toward the engineering side — writing ETL pipelines in Python, leaning on DuckDB for the fast stuff, and trying to understand what makes a data pipeline hold up versus just barely run. The fun part is always the question underneath the data.
